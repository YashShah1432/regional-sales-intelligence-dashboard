# Regional Sales Intelligence Dashboard | Python EDA & Power BI Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The Regional Sales Intelligence Dashboard is an end-to-end analytics project that combines Python-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Power BI dashboarding to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

The project evaluates sales performance across products, customers, sales channels, and geographic regions to uncover revenue drivers, profitability trends, and growth opportunities. Through interactive visualizations and detailed analysis, stakeholders can make informed business decisions and optimize overall performance.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Analyze regional sales data to identify the key drivers of revenue, profit, and profit margins across products, customers, sales channels, and geographic regions. Uncover seasonal sales patterns, customer purchasing behavior, and regional performance variations while highlighting top- and bottom-performing entities. Use these insights to optimize product strategy, improve channel effectiveness, strengthen customer relationships, and support data-driven decisions for sustainable business growth and profitability.

---

Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Power BI | Microsoft Excel
